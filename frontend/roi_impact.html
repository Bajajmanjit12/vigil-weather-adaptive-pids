METHODOLOGY

How VIGIL Estimates Impact

01

Historical Readings

VIGIL evaluates the environmental readings recorded in its decision history.

02

Static Baseline

The baseline considers a high-sensitivity policy where disruptive weather conditions can generate alarms.

03

Dynamic Recommendation

VIGIL compares the baseline against its weather-aware dynamic sensitivity recommendation.

04

Impact Estimate

The difference between the two policies is converted into an estimated false-alarm reduction percentage.

IMPACT CALCULATION

Estimated False-Alarm Reduction

Static Alarms − Dynamic Alarms ÷ Static Alarms × 100

This is an illustrative simulation metric based on the available VIGIL decision history. It should not be interpreted as a certified field-performance measurement.

i
Interpretation note

The current backend calculates an estimated false-alarm reduction from recorded readings. Financial savings, manpower savings, maintenance costs, and payback period are not currently calculated by VIGIL.
